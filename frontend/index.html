Reading the acoustic view
Color scale
Dark = low energy · bright coral = high energy. The scale is fixed (not per-image auto-scaled), so similar energy always shows the same color across windows.
Frequency axis
Perceptual (mel) scale — speech harmonics between 0–3 kHz are given more visual space than the 3–8 kHz range.
Model focus
The hatched band shows the time region the detection model weighted most heavily. It reflects temporal attention — not a specific frequency artifact.
Centroid
The frequency that splits the spectrum into two equal energy halves. Voiced speech typically sits 0.5–2 kHz.
Rolloff
The frequency below which 85% of energy is concentrated.
Flatness
0 = pure tone (harmonic), 1 = noise-like. Describes texture, not authenticity.
Dominant band
The frequency range with the highest mean energy in this window.
